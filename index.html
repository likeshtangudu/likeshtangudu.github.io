How I think through problems

Case 01 — From user pain point to product direction

I start by identifying the real friction in the user journey, not just the visible symptom. Then I frame the problem with user, business, and operational lenses before narrowing the opportunity into a clear product direction.

Case 02 — Turning ambiguity into execution

Whether in consulting or startup settings, I work by breaking complex goals into measurable workstreams, aligning stakeholders, and creating clarity around outcomes, ownership, and speed.

Case 03 — Balancing intuition with analytics

I value strong instincts, but I trust evidence. My approach combines market context, user insight, and data-backed reasoning to make decisions that are both thoughtful and practical.
